Our story

In 2011, Maya Ellison sold the logistics company she had spent fourteen years building. Within a month, three other business owners had asked her to look at their companies. Northstar grew from those conversations.

We have stayed deliberately small. Every consultant has run a business or a major part of one, and every client works directly with a partner.

Our office is in downtown Austin, but our clients include dental groups in Denver, contractors in Ohio and restaurants across Texas.

How we work

Three principles we hold ourselves to on every project.

Numbers first

We start with your data, not a template. Recommendations are tied to figures you can check.

Plain language

No jargon, no 80-slide decks. You get a plan your front-line team can read and act on.

Leave it better

We train your people as we go, so the improvements continue after we leave.
