Questions owners ask us

Don’t see yours? Send us a message and we will reply within one business day.

What size of business do you work with?

Mostly owner-led companies with 10 to 200 employees and $1M–$30M in revenue. If you are smaller, our Growth strategy package is a good first step.

How long does a typical engagement last?

Most projects run 3 to 9 months. We scope everything up front with a fixed fee, so there are no surprise invoices.

Do you only work with businesses in Austin?

No. About half our clients are elsewhere in the US. We run workshops on-site when it matters and work remotely the rest of the time.

What happens on the first call?

We ask about your goals and what is getting in the way. If we are a good fit, you get a written proposal within three business days. If not, we will point you to someone who is.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the work: if a deliverable misses the scope we agreed, we fix it at no cost. We share results openly, but outcomes depend on your team putting the plan to work.
